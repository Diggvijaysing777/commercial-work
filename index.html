Our Story

Born in the
Mountains, For You

We're not just a travel agency — we're travellers who fell in love with India and decided to share it. Founded in the hills of Himachal Pradesh in 2012, we've since grown into a team that crafts journeys across the whole country — from the high Himalayas to the deserts of Rajasthan.

That's why our trips go places package tours don't. We know the shepherd who runs the best homestay above Kaza, the boatman for a dawn Ganga aarti in Varanasi, and the haveli in Jaisalmer worth changing your whole plan for.
