Our Story

Born in the
Mountains, For You

We're not a travel agency — we're mountain people who fell in love with Himachal Pradesh and decided to share it. Founded in Shimla in 2012, every team member has grown up walking these trails, eating in local dhabas, and sleeping under these stars.

That's why our trips go places no package tour does. We know the shepherd who runs the best homestay above Kaza. We know which sunrise over Rohtang is worth the 4AM start, and which shortcut saves you three hours on the Pin-Bhaba crossing.
